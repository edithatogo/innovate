Use this atlas to identify a model family and the names used by the native API.
The cards combine a short reading guide with metadata generated directly from
the Rust discovery contract. Start with the [quickstart](/innovate/user-guide/getting-started/)
to learn the table boundary, then use the [tutorial chooser](/innovate/tutorials/)
to find a worked task.

## Choose the scientific question first

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A level or a share over time">
    Bass, Logistic, Gompertz and Fisher–Pry offer different trajectory shapes and
    parameter interpretations. State the measured quantity and units before
    comparing their predictions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Generations and interacting products">
    Norton–Bass, Lotka–Volterra, multi-product competition, complementary goods
    and lock-in expose different interaction conventions. Read the variant
    definition before interpreting a change in one component.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A model wrapped around another path">
    Policy hazard, latent process, regime switching, hierarchical and mixture
    models add different structure. Their names do not establish an inferential
    method, calibrated uncertainty or a causal interpretation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dynamics and simulation engines">
    Composite ODE trajectories, deterministic network diffusion and Bass Kairos
    simulation are distinct interfaces. A model key, an engine and an output
    quantity are separate choices.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Read the capability metadata carefully

<Aside type="caution" title="Advertised metadata is a starting point">
  Discovery lists describe advertised capabilities. They are not results from
  an execution or scientific review, and independent lists of models, variants,
  engines and operations do not establish support for every combination.
  In particular, a family-level rate flag does not resolve the behavior of a
  specific variant and quantity. Check the [native conventions](/innovate/native-migration/)
  and the relevant executable example before using that combination.
</Aside>

An absent variant list entry means that discovery declares no named variant;
it does not define an extra `default` variant. The key `network_diffusion`
describes a deterministic spillover model and is separate from the
`kairos_network` engine advertised for `bass`.

## Turn a model choice into an analysis

Read the [fitting guide](/innovate/user-guide/fitting/) for estimation and
diagnostics, and [prediction and forecasting](/innovate/user-guide/forecasting/)
for levels, rates and time units. A runnable synthetic example checks its
declared identities and failure behavior; empirical fit and scientific
interpretation require evidence for the data and question at hand.

The inventory is built from
[`discovery.json`](https://github.com/edithatogo/innovate/blob/main/rust/crates/innovate-core/src/discovery.json).
The [documentation quality guide](/innovate/maintainers/documentation-quality/)
explains how source, execution and scientific-review evidence are distinguished.
